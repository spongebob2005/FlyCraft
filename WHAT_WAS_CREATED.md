## 🚀 Getting Started

### For Users
1. Download `flycraft-1.0.jar`
2. Place in `.minecraft/mods/`
3. Launch Minecraft with Forge
4. Press V, C, B, X, or Z for animations
5. Enjoy!

### For Developers
1. See `DOCUMENTATION_INDEX.md`
2. Review `ANIMATION_IMPLEMENTATION.md`
3. Check `ANIMATION_EXAMPLES.md`
4. Modify and extend as needed
